HOW TO PLAY

Think beyond the arithmetic.

  1. Hide a nine-cell equation. Use digits 0–9 and + − × ÷ = ^. Repetition is allowed. Secrets must balance; no leading zeros or adjacent symbols.
  2. Probe any nine symbols. Guesses can be mathematically invalid. The goal is the exact secret, including the positions of symbols.
  3. Read the colours. Green is exact; yellow appears elsewhere; red has no remaining match. Matching consumes each occurrence once, with greens taking priority.
  4. Keep narrowing it down. Solo play has unlimited attempts. In a duel, both players submit once per round before clues appear. A correct guess wins; two correct guesses in the same round produce a tie and a replay.
Arithmetic in this version

Powers first, then multiplication/division, then addition/subtraction. Powers associate right to left. Multiple equals signs may form a valid chain. Parentheses and unary minus are excluded. 0^0 is not accepted; exponents are bounded for browser performance.

Local duels use private handoff screens. Look away during your opponent’s turn. Optional five-minute clocks apply to guesses; secret creation is untimed. Timers continue when the tab is hidden.
