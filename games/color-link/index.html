    HOW TO PLAY

    Make room. Make a move.

    1. Place the pieces. The opening turn uses one piece. Later turns use two different colours from the shared A–N pool. Each colour has two pieces.
    2. Leave a connection. Every pair must be connectable by a separate path of at least six squares, counting both endpoints. Move horizontally or vertically. Paths cannot share squares; empty squares may remain.
    3. Call “Impossible.” After an opponent’s move, challenge instead of placing. The opponent must add any missing mates and draw every connection, including fixed X/Y pairs.
    4. Prove it to win it. A complete valid proof earns the defender a point. A concession or expired proof timer gives the challenger a point. First to three wins.
    Drawing a proof

    Place any missing mates first. Start on a piece and drag—or click adjacent squares—to its partner. Click an earlier square to backtrack. Select a colour and choose “Clear selected path” to redraw it. You can reposition mates you added during the proof.

    Optional timing: 3 minutes per placement and 5 per proof. An unfinished placement loses the round. Timers continue while help is open or the tab is in the background.
