    HOW TO PLAY

    The move after the move.

    1. Plan in secret. Arrange your movement cards in order. The starter uses all five; the second player chooses four for their first sequence. After that, both use five.
    2. Reveal, then move. Both opening cards are revealed together. On your turn, move one piece using your current card. The diagram marks legal destinations relative to that piece. You can jump over intervening pieces, but cannot land on your own.
    3. Capture or invade. Landing on an enemy captures it. Win by capturing the king, or moving your king onto the opponent’s starting king square. Small pieces cannot win by reaching that square.
    4. Keep the sequence moving. Reveal your next card after moving. If your card has no legal move, skip it. When your sequence ends, immediately arrange all five cards and reveal your first before your opponent moves.
    5. Win twice. The starting player alternates between rounds. The first player to win two rounds wins the match.
    Optional clock

    You have 90 seconds to move. If it expires, your opponent chooses a legal move for one of your pieces using your current card. Card planning is untimed in this adaptation.
