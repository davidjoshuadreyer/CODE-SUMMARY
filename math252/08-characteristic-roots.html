Constant coefficients and characteristic roots

Turn differentiation into a polynomial, then translate its roots into real solutions.

Before you start: Quadratic equations, exponentials, sine, and cosine.

THE IDEA

Understand before calculating

Why a polynomial appears

For ay′′+by′+cy=0ay''+by'+cy=0ay′′+by′+cy=0, try y=erxy=e^{rx}y=erx. Substitution gives erx(ar2+br+c)=0.e^{rx}(ar^2+br+c)=0.erx(ar2+br+c)=0. Since the exponential is nonzero, solve the characteristic equation ar2+br+c=0ar^2+br+c=0ar2+br+c=0.

Complex roots α±iβ\alpha\pm i\betaα±iβ give eαx(C1cos⁡βx+C2sin⁡βx).e^{\alpha x}(C_1\cos\beta x+C_2\sin\beta x).eαx(C1​cosβx+C2​sinβx). For higher-order equations, use all roots, with powers of xxx up to one less than each multiplicity.

Your method checklist

  1. Write the characteristic polynomial with the correct signs.
  2. Find all roots and identify their pattern.
  3. Write the complete real solution.
  4. Differentiate before applying derivative initial conditions.

A repeated root
