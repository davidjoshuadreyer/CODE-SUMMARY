A map of the course

Use this topic guide to choose where to begin, then work through the corresponding notes and exercises.

1 · First-order equations

Sections 1.1–3.1. Identify the equation’s form before choosing separation, an integrating factor, exactness, or substitution. Use an initial condition to determine the constant.

2 · Higher-order equations

Sections 4.1–5.1. Build the homogeneous solution, find a particular solution when needed, and apply initial conditions. Connect the result to mechanical motion and circuits.

3 · Power series

Sections 6.1–6.2. Express the unknown solution as a series, align powers, and compare coefficients to obtain a recurrence.

4 · Laplace transforms

Sections 7.1–7.5. Transform an initial-value problem into algebra, solve for the transformed function, and invert it. Review partial fractions and shifting properties.

5 · Linear systems

Sections 8.1–8.3. Write coupled equations in matrix form and use eigenvalues and eigenvectors to construct solutions. Account for forcing in nonhomogeneous systems.
