How do I recognize the method?

Try these checks. Some equations fit more than one method; choose a valid setup, not just a familiar label.

  • Separable: Can the right side become an x-only factor times a y-only factor? Move them to opposite sides.
  • Linear: First divide by the coefficient of y′. Can you write y′ + P(x)y = f(x), with no y², sin(y), or other nonlinear y terms? Use an integrating factor.
  • Exact: In M dx + N dy = 0, check Mᵧ = Nₓ. The form alone is not proof.
  • Homogeneous: Does the right side depend only on y/x? Try y = vx and y′ = v + xv′.
  • Bernoulli: Look for y′ + P(x)y = f(x)yⁿ. Use u = y¹⁻ⁿ, for n ≠ 0, 1.
  • Combination: A repeated ax + by + c suggests u = ax + by + c; then u′ = a + by′.
  • Word problems: “Proportional to amount” → exponential model. Cooling → temperature difference. Mixing → rate in − rate out.

An initial condition determines a constant after solving. It does not tell you which method to use. When dividing, check excluded zero solutions and domain restrictions.
