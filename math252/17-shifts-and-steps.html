Shifts, switches, and piecewise inputs

Distinguish exponential shifts from time delays and write switched inputs correctly.

Before you start: Basic Laplace transforms and piecewise functions.

THE IDEA

Understand before calculating

An exponential shifts s

If L{f(t)}=F(s)\mathcal L\{f(t)\}=F(s)L{f(t)}=F(s), then L{eatf(t)}=F(s−a).\mathcal L\{e^{at}f(t)\}=F(s-a).L{eatf(t)}=F(s−a). Replace every occurrence of sss in the transform, not just the denominator’s first term.

Your method checklist

  1. Decide whether the change is an exponential factor or a time delay.
  2. For a delayed input, rewrite everything inside using t−a.
  3. Apply the appropriate transform rule.
  4. When inverting, delay the entire function and multiply by the step.
