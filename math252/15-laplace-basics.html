The Laplace transform

Understand the transform as a new representation of a function and learn the core transform pairs.

Before you start: Improper integrals and exponential functions.

UnderstandMethodExample 1Your turnAvoid this mistake

THE IDEA

Understand before calculating

A function of t becomes a function of s

The one-sided Laplace transform is F(s)=L{f(t)}=∫0∞e−stf(t) dt.F(s)=\mathcal L\{f(t)\}=\int_0^{\infty}e^{-st}f(t)\,dt.F(s)=L{f(t)}=∫0∞​e−stf(t)dt. The integral must converge. For example, exponential growth in ttt restricts how large sss must be.

Core pairs

L{1}=1s,L{tn}=n!sn+1,L{eat}=1s−a.\mathcal L\{1\}=\frac1s,\quad \mathcal L\{t^n\}=\frac{n!}{s^{n+1}},\quad \mathcal L\{e^{at}\}=\frac1{s-a}.L{1}=s1​,L{tn}=sn+1n!​,L{eat}=s−a1​. The power rule uses a nonnegative integer nnn. For sine and cosine, L{sin⁡bt}=bs2+b2,L{cos⁡bt}=ss2+b2.\mathcal L\{\sin bt\}=\frac{b}{s^2+b^2},\qquad \mathcal L\{\cos bt\}=\frac{s}{s^2+b^2}.L{sinbt}=s2+b2b​,L{cosbt}=s2+b2s​. Use s>0s>0s>0 for the polynomial and trigonometric pairs, and s>as>as>a for eate^{at}eat.

Linearity, not multiplication

L{af+bg}=aF+bG\mathcal L\{af+bg\}=aF+bGL{af+bg}=aF+bG. But the transform of a product is generally not the product of the transforms. Keep ttt-domain functions and sss-domain expressions on their correct sides.

  2. Match each term to a transform pair.
  3. Keep coefficients and factorials.
  4. State a convergence region when asked.
