Linear theory and reduction of order

Understand why solutions combine, and find a second solution when one is already known.

Before you start: Product rule, integration, and first-order equations.

Your method checklist

  1. Normalize the equation and identify its homogeneous part.
  2. For reduction of order, set y=vy₁ and compute both derivatives.
  3. Substitute, cancel, and solve for v′.
  4. Integrate once more and keep a genuinely independent solution.
