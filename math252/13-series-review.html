Power series essentials

Read sigma notation, differentiate a series, and line up powers before comparing coefficients.

Before you start: Polynomials, factorials, and the ratio test.

THE IDEA

Understand before calculating

An infinite polynomial

A power series centered at zero is y=∑n=0∞anxn=a0+a1x+a2x2+⋯ .y=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_nx^n=a_0+a_1x+a_2x^2+\cdots.y=n=0∑∞​an​xn=a0​+a1​x+a2​x2+⋯. Inside its radius of convergence you may differentiate and integrate term by term. The coefficients encode derivatives: an=y(n)(0)/n!a_n=y^{(n)}(0)/n!an​=y(n)(0)/n!.

Differentiate without losing the starting index

y′=∑n=1∞nanxn−1,y′′=∑n=2∞n(n−1)anxn−2.y'=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}na_nx^{n-1},\qquad y''=\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}n(n-1)a_nx^{n-2}.y′=n=1∑∞​nan​xn−1,y′′=n=2∑∞​n(n−1)an​xn−2. The constant term vanishes in y′y'y′, and both the constant and linear terms vanish in y′′y''y′′.

Reindex to the same power

For coefficient matching, rewrite y′′=∑n=0∞(n+2)(n+1)an+2xn.y''=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(n+2)(n+1)a_{n+2}x^n.y′′=n=0∑∞​(n+2)(n+1)an+2​xn. Changing the index changes the coefficient, the exponent, and the lower bound together.

Know a few anchor series

ex=∑n=0∞xnn!,sin⁡x=∑n=0∞(−1)nx2n+1(2n+1)!,cos⁡x=∑n=0∞(−1)nx2n(2n)!.e^x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{n!},\quad \sin x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!},\quad \cos x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n}}{(2n)!}.ex=n=0∑∞​n!xn​,sinx=n=0∑∞​(2n+1)!(−1)nx2n+1​,cosx=n=0∑∞​(2n)!(−1)nx2n​. These converge for all real xxx. The geometric series ∑xn=1/(1−x)\sum x^n=1/(1-x)∑xn=1/(1−x) instead requires ∣x∣<1|x|<1∣x∣<1.

Your method checklist

  1. Write a few terms to see what the summation means.
  2. Differentiate or integrate term by term inside the convergence interval.
  3. Reindex every series to matching powers.
  4. Check convergence separately, including endpoints when needed.
