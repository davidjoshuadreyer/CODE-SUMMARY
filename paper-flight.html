The little science behind the flight ＋

This is a playful glider model: lift acts across the airflow, drag slows the plane, and weight pulls it down. Both lift and drag grow with airspeed squared. Pull up to trade speed for height; ease the nose down to recover from a stall. The glowing columns are thermals: rising air that helps you stay aloft.

Wingspan, mass, trim, and upgrades feed the simulation. Distances and forces are tuned for satisfying short flights, rather than predicting a real paper plane. Inspired by NASA’s glider aerodynamics.

Your designs, upgrades, and flight log are saved on this device. No hurry. The sky will be here.
