LAB 06 NOTEBOOK

First Order RC–RL

Compare short and long half-periods. Use vC for the RC time constant and vR for RL.

PREPARATION / PREDICTED

τRC = RC · τRL = L/R
With losses: τRC = (R + Rgen)C
τRL = L/(R + Rcoil + Rgen)
Cursor τ = ΔX / ln(Vmax/Vmin)

PROCEDURE

Analytic periodic steady state, not the first startup cycle. The 50 Ω source and 70 Ω winding are handout assumptions; replace winding resistance with your measured value. Component tolerances, scope noise and probe loading are not simulated. Records are simulated practice data, not your physical lab results.
