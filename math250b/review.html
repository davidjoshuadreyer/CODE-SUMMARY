  • Test 1 topics: lessons 1–3 for partial derivatives, chain rule, tangent planes, errors, and critical points.
  • Test 2 topics: lessons 2–5 for gradients, constraints, and multiple-integral bounds.
  • Test 3 topics: lessons 5–7 for spherical coordinates, transformations, surface area, and work.
  • Assignment 4 and formula-sheet extension: lessons 7–8 for conservative fields and integral theorems.
