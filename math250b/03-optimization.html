03 · Optimization and constraints

Classify critical points, check boundaries, and solve Lagrange multiplier equations.

Find all candidates

For a differentiable function on an open region, interior extrema require fx=fy=0f_x=f_y=0fx​=fy​=0. Solve these equations together. Critical points can also occur where a partial derivative does not exist. A stationary point is a candidate, not a guarantee of a maximum or minimum.

Absolute extrema require boundary checks

A continuous function on a closed, bounded region attains an absolute maximum and minimum. Compare function values at interior critical points, critical points along each boundary segment, and corners or endpoints. Substitute each boundary equation into fff to reduce that part to one variable.

Add the constraint equation

For a regular constraint g(x,y)=cg(x,y)=cg(x,y)=c, solve ∇f=λ∇g,g=c.\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g,\qquad g=c.∇f=λ∇g,g=c. Include every real solution and compare objective values. Check separately any constraint points where ∇g=0\nabla g=0∇g=0, plus endpoints or other restrictions. Dividing by a variable can accidentally discard a zero-valued candidate.
