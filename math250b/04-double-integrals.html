04 · Double integrals and polar coordinates

Describe a region, choose an integration order, and compute area, volume, or mass.

Bounds describe slices

For vertical slices with a≤x≤ba\le x\le ba≤x≤b and g1(x)≤y≤g2(x)g_1(x)\le y\le g_2(x)g1​(x)≤y≤g2​(x), write ∬Rf dA=∫ab∫g1(x)g2(x)f(x,y) dy dx.\iint_R f\,dA=\int_a^b\int_{g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)}f(x,y)\,dy\,dx.∬R​fdA=∫ab​∫g1​(x)g2​(x)​f(x,y)dydx. Integrate the inner variable first. Horizontal slices reverse these roles. Sketch the region and identify intersections before choosing bounds.

Changing the order means changing the bounds, not just swapping the differential symbols.

Use polar coordinates for circular geometry

Choose the physical integrand

Area uses integrand 1. Volume between surfaces uses top minus bottom.
