1 · Setup and paper thickness

Measure a stack of 100 sheets. A difference of 200 page numbers means 100 sheets. Enter the actual sheet count. The manual specifies ±0.010 mm micrometer uncertainty.

Measure the fixed plate dimensions for Part B. Suggested size: 20 cm × 20 cm. Enter actual measurements.

Automatic uncertainties

Meter: Circuit-Test DCM-200 (confirmed from your photo). Selected range: 2000 pF.

Ruler default: ±0.1 cm for a millimetre scale, including alignment at both ends (manual rule 4b). Adjust for your ruler. The supplied meter specification is ±(1% of reading + 3 digits). Enter the last-digit increment in pF for the range you are using: ΔC = 0.01 × C + 3 × increment. Change the settings if your range has a different specification. Select the last-digit estimate only when no specification is available. A typed uncertainty overrides the automatic value; clear it to return to automatic. Check these estimates against the equipment you use.

Fits use a free intercept. Slope uncertainty uses half the maximum–minimum range from the instructor template’s envelope method. Browser graphs are previews; download each original Excel graph below for your final figures.

5 · Reference and discussion

The reference is prefilled with OpenStax’s nominal paper value of 3.7. Its table gives no uncertainty, so zero is used only for the numerical comparison. Change it if your instructor gives a different reference. If a source gives a range, use its midpoint and half-range, and explain this in the source field. Use zero only when treating a reference value as exact.
