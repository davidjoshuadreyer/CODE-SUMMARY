Question 129.25 · Motional voltage along a rod

Rod a→b points up-right, length 0.300 m. It moves down-right perpendicular to itself at 5.00 m/s through B = 0.450 T into page. Find voltage magnitude, higher-potential end, internal E and positive end; then voltage for motion along the rod or out of the page.

Original questionOpen the original D2L handout

Charge separation balances qE against q(v × B).

Find voltage and electric field

∣ΔV∣=BLv=(0.450)(0.300)(5.00)=0.675 VE=vB=(5.00)(0.450)=2.25 V/mqE+qv×B=0⇒E=−v×B\begin{gathered}|\Delta V|=BLv=(0.450)(0.300)(5.00)=0.675\ \mathrm V\\E=vB=(5.00)(0.450)=2.25\ \mathrm{V/m}\\q\mathbf E+q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B=0\quad\Rightarrow\quad\mathbf E=-\mathbf v\times\mathbf B\end{gathered}∣ΔV∣=BLv=(0.450)(0.300)(5.00)=0.675 VE=vB=(5.00)(0.450)=2.25 V/mqE+qv×B=0⇒E=−v×B​

Check the other motions

v∥rod:(v×B)⋅ℓ=0v∥B:v×B=0ΔV=0for both alternate motions\begin{gathered}\mathbf v\parallel\text{rod}:\quad(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)\cdot\boldsymbol\ell=0\\\mathbf v\parallel\mathbf B:\quad\mathbf v\times\mathbf B=0\\\Delta V=0\quad\text{for both alternate motions}\end{gathered}v∥rod:(v×B)⋅ℓ=0v∥B:v×B=0ΔV=0for both alternate motions​

Final answer

0.675 V; b positive and higher; E = 2.25 V/m toward a; both alternate motions give 0 V.

For along-rod motion the magnetic force is perpendicular to the rod; for motion parallel/antiparallel to B it vanishes.
