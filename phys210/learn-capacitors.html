1. Define capacitance from geometry

Capacitance C=Q/ΔVC=Q/\Delta VC=Q/ΔV (farads, F) uses the positive charge magnitude Q on one plate and voltage magnitude between plates. For parallel plates, C=εA/dC=\varepsilon A/dC=εA/d neglecting edges; A is overlap area and d is separation. For concentric spherical conductors with radii a < b, C=4πεab/(b−a)C=4\pi\varepsilon ab/(b-a)C=4πεab/(b−a). For long coaxial cylinders of length ℓ and radii a < b, C=2πεℓ/ln⁡(b/a)C=2\pi\varepsilon\ell/\ln(b/a)C=2πεℓ/ln(b/a). Here ε=κε0\varepsilon=\kappa\varepsilon_0ε=κε0​ for a fully filling linear dielectric.
