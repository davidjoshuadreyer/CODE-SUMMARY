QUESTION 1 · Homework 6 & 7 · 24.7

Changing capacitor separation

Exact question and original solution screenshots ↗

  1. Identify

    At fixed charge, doubling separation halves capacitance.

  2. Set up

    C=Q/V=5.71 pF; d=ε0A/C; V′=Q/(C/2)=2VC=Q/V=5.71\ \mathrm{pF};\ d=\varepsilon_0 A/C;\ V'=Q/(C/2)=2VC=Q/V=5.71 pF; d=ε0​A/C; V′=Q/(C/2)=2V.

  3. Execute

    d=(8.854×10−12)(6.80×10−4)/(5.714×10−12)=1.05×10−3 m; V′=84.0 Vd=(8.854\times10^{-12})(6.80\times10^{-4})/(5.714\times10^{-12})=1.05\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm m;\ V'=84.0\ \mathrm Vd=(8.854×10−12)(6.80×10−4)/(5.714×10−12)=1.05×10−3 m; V′=84.0 V.

  4. Evaluate

    The field stays constant because Q/A is unchanged; voltage grows with gap. This question appears in both Homework 6 and 7.

d = 1.05 mm; new voltage 84.0 V.

  2. Set up

  3. Execute

    dmin=0.0100 m; rmin=0.0424 m; Qmax=5.00×10−10 Cd_{min}=0.0100\ \mathrm m;\ r_{min}=0.0424\ \mathrm m;\ Q_{max}=5.00\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm Cdmin​=0.0100 m; rmin​=0.0424 m; Qmax​=5.00×10−10 C.

QUESTION 3 · Homework 7 · 24.24

Work pulling plates apart

Exact question and original solution screenshots ↗

  1. Identify

    Use U = Q²/(2C), since charge is fixed.

  2. Set up

    Vi=Q/C; Cf=C/2; Vf=2Vi; Wext=Uf−Ui=Q2/(2C)V_i=Q/C;\ C_f=C/2;\ V_f=2V_i;\ W_{ext}=U_f-U_i=Q^2/(2C)Vi​=Q/C; Cf​=C/2; Vf​=2Vi​; Wext​=Uf​−Ui​=Q2/(2C).

  3. Execute

    Vi=4.24 kV; Vf=8.48 kV; Wext=8.27×10−3 JV_i=4.24\ \mathrm{kV};\ V_f=8.48\ \mathrm{kV};\ W_{ext}=8.27\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm JVi​=4.24 kV; Vf​=8.48 kV; Wext​=8.27×10−3 J.

  4. Evaluate

    Positive work separates attracting plates; the energy doubles.

4.24 kV → 8.48 kV; external work 8.27 mJ.
