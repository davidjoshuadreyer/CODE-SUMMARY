QUESTION 1 · Homework 7 · 24.66

Half-filled dielectric capacitor

Exact question and original solution screenshots ↗

  1. Identify

    Side-by-side areas are parallel capacitors at the same voltage.

  2. Set up

    C=ε0A(1+κ)/(2d); U=CV2/2; C0=ε0A/dC=\varepsilon_0 A(1+\kappa)/(2d);\ U=CV^2/2;\ C_0=\varepsilon_0 A/dC=ε0​A(1+κ)/(2d); U=CV2/2; C0​=ε0​A/d.

  3. Execute

    C=62.3 pF; U=1.01×10−8 JC=62.3\ \mathrm{pF};\ U=1.01\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm JC=62.3 pF; U=1.01×10−8 J. After removal C0=28.3 pFC_0=28.3\ \mathrm{pF}C0​=28.3 pF and U0=4.59×10−9 JU_0=4.59\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm JU0​=4.59×10−9 J.

  4. Evaluate

    The battery fixes voltage, not charge. Using a series-dielectric formula would describe a different geometry.

62.3 pF; 10.1 nJ initially; 4.59 nJ after removal.

QUESTION 2 · Homework 4 · 22.21

Uniformly charged insulating sphere

Exact question and original solution screenshots ↗

  1. Identify

    Use spherical symmetry. Outside the sphere all its charge is enclosed. Inside a uniformly charged insulator, enclosed charge grows as r³; the electric field need not be zero.

  2. Set up

  3. Execute

    rout=0.355+0.145=0.500 m,∣Q∣=4.87×10−8 Cr_{out}=0.355+0.145=0.500\ \mathrm m,\quad |Q|=4.87\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm Crout​=0.355+0.145=0.500 m,∣Q∣=4.87×10−8 C (a) ∣ρ∣=2.60×10−7 C/m3(a)\ |\rho|=2.60\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{C/m^3}(a) ∣ρ∣=2.60×10−7 C/m3 (b) ∣E(0.200)∣=1.96×103 N/C(b)\ |E(0.200)|=1.96\times10^3\ \mathrm{N/C}(b) ∣E(0.200)∣=1.96×103 N/C
