QUESTION 1 · Tutorial 7 · 4

Unknown capacitor from two initial voltages

  2. Solve for C

C≈22 μF(C)C\approx22\ \mu\mathrm F\quad\text{(C)}C≈22 μF(C)

Both sides give 1687.5 μC at the unrounded C. Agrees with the printed key.

QUESTION 2 · Tutorial 7 · 5

Unknown initially uncharged capacitor

Exact question and original solution screenshots ↗

  1. Conserve charge

    The initially uncharged capacitor contributes no initial node charge. (30)(80)=(30+C)(20)[C in μF](30)(80)=(30+C)(20)\qquad[C\text{ in }\mu\mathrm F](30)(80)=(30+C)(20)[C in μF]

  2. Solve the total capacitance

    The fourfold decrease in voltage requires a fourfold increase in total capacitance. 30+C=120⇒C=90 μF30+C=120\quad\Rightarrow\quad C=90\ \mu\mathrm F30+C=120⇒C=90 μF
C=90 μF(D)C=90\ \mu\mathrm F\quad\text{(D)}C=90 μF(D)

Final charges are 600 μC and 1800 μC, totaling the original 2400 μC. Agrees with the key.
