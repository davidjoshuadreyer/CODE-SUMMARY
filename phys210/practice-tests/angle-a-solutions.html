QUESTION 1 · Homework 4 · 22.33

Sphere suspended beside a charged sheet

Exact question and original solution screenshots ↗

  1. Identify

    The positive sphere is attracted toward the negative sheet. Three forces balance: tension along the thread, weight downward, and electric force toward the sheet.

  2. Set up

    For a very large insulating sheet, E=∣σ∣/(2ε0)E=|\sigma|/(2\varepsilon_0)E=∣σ∣/(2ε0​). Tsin⁡θ=qE,Tcos⁡θ=mg,tan⁡θ=q∣σ∣2ε0mgT\sin\theta=qE,\quad T\cos\theta=mg,\quad \tan\theta=\frac{q|\sigma|}{2\varepsilon_0 mg}Tsinθ=qE,Tcosθ=mg,tanθ=2ε0​mgq∣σ∣​

  3. Execute

    E=141.2 N/C,Fe=7.06×10−6 N,mg=3.92×10−5 NE=141.2\ \mathrm{N/C},\quad F_e=7.06\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm N,\quad mg=3.92\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm NE=141.2 N/C,Fe​=7.06×10−6 N,mg=3.92×10−5 N θ=tan⁡−1(0.1801)=10.2∘\theta=\tan^{-1}(0.1801)=10.2^\circθ=tan−1(0.1801)=10.2∘

  4. Evaluate

    The sphere leans toward the sheet. Use the insulating-sheet field σ/(2ε₀), not the conductor-surface value σ/ε₀. This is manual problem 22.31, despite the handout numbering 22.33.

θ = 10.2° toward the negative sheet.
