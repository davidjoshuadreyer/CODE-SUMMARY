QUESTION 2 · Homework 7 · 24.66

Half-filled dielectric capacitor

  1. Identify

    Side-by-side areas are parallel capacitors at the same voltage.

  2. Set up

    C=ε0A(1+κ)/(2d); U=CV2/2; C0=ε0A/dC=\varepsilon_0 A(1+\kappa)/(2d);\ U=CV^2/2;\ C_0=\varepsilon_0 A/dC=ε0​A(1+κ)/(2d); U=CV2/2; C0​=ε0​A/d.

  3. Execute

    C=62.3 pF; U=1.01×10−8 JC=62.3\ \mathrm{pF};\ U=1.01\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm JC=62.3 pF; U=1.01×10−8 J. After removal C0=28.3 pFC_0=28.3\ \mathrm{pF}C0​=28.3 pF and U0=4.59×10−9 JU_0=4.59\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm JU0​=4.59×10−9 J.

  4. Evaluate

    The battery fixes voltage, not charge. Using a series-dielectric formula would describe a different geometry.
