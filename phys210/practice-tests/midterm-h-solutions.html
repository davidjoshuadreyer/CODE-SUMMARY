QUESTION 1 · Homework 5 · 23.7

Two protons approaching head-on

Exact question and original solution screenshots ↗

  1. Identify

    Both protons contribute kinetic energy, and both stop momentarily at closest approach.

  2. Set up

    Ktotal=2(12mpv2)=mpv2=ke2/rmin; Fmax=ke2/rmin2K_{total}=2(\tfrac12m_pv^2)=m_pv^2=k e^2/r_{min};\ F_{max}=ke^2/r_{min}^2Ktotal​=2(21​mp​v2)=mp​v2=ke2/rmin​; Fmax​=ke2/rmin2​.

  3. Execute

    rmin=3.45×10−12 m; Fmax=1.94×10−5 Nr_{min}=3.45\times10^{-12}\ \mathrm m;\ F_{max}=1.94\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm Nrmin​=3.45×10−12 m; Fmax​=1.94×10−5 N.

  4. Evaluate

    Using only one proton’s kinetic energy would give twice the separation and one-quarter the force.

Maximum force 1.94 × 10⁻⁵ N.
