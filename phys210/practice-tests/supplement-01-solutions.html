QUESTION 2 · Homework 5 · 23.1

Work along a two-dimensional move

  1. Identify

    Electric work equals initial minus final potential energy.

  2. Set up

    ri=0.150, rf=0.2502+0.2502; W=kq1q2(1/ri−1/rf)r_i=0.150,\ r_f=\sqrt{0.250^2+0.250^2};\ W=kq_1q_2(1/r_i-1/r_f)ri​=0.150, rf​=0.2502+0.2502​; W=kq1​q2​(1/ri​−1/rf​).

    rf=0.3536 m; W=(8.988×109)(2.40×10−6)(−4.30×10−6)(6.667−2.828)=−0.356 Jr_f=0.3536\ \mathrm m;\ W=(8.988\times10^9)(2.40\times10^{-6})(-4.30\times10^{-6})(6.667-2.828)=-0.356\ \mathrm Jrf​=0.3536 m; W=(8.988×109)(2.40×10−6)(−4.30×10−6)(6.667−2.828)=−0.356 J.

    Moving unlike charges apart raises potential energy; electric work is negative.

QUESTION 3 · Homework 6 · 24.1

  1. Identify

    Use the field between two oppositely charged plates.

  2. Set up

    V=Ed; A=Q/(ε0E); C=Q/VV=Ed;\ A=Q/(\varepsilon_0 E);\ C=Q/VV=Ed; A=Q/(ε0​E); C=Q/V.

  3. Execute
