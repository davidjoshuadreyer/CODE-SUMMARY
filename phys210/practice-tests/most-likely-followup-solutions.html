QUESTION 4 · Tutorial 4 · 7

Exact question and original solution screenshots ↗

  1. Track induced charges

    The inner surface has −4.0 pC so the metal’s field vanishes. The outer surface then has +8.0 pC.

  2. Find total enclosed charge

    The external Gaussian sphere contains point charge plus shell net charge. Qenc=4.0+4.0=8.0 pCQ_{\rm enc}=4.0+4.0=8.0\,\mathrm{pC}Qenc​=4.0+4.0=8.0pC

  3. Calculate the exterior field
