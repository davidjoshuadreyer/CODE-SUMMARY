Charge and Coulomb’s law

1. What charge means

Charge is a signed property measured in coulombs (C). Electrons have charge −e-e−e and protons +e+e+e, where e=1.602×10−19 Ce=1.602\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm Ce=1.602×10−19C. Charge is conserved: rubbing transfers charge rather than creating it. In a conductor, some charges move easily; in an insulator they remain localized. Grounding allows charge to move to or from Earth.

2. Calculate one interaction

This is a magnitude. Draw attraction for opposite signs and repulsion for matching signs. Convert centimetres to metres and microcoulombs to coulombs before substituting.
