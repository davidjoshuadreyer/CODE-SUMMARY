1. Inductance stores magnetic energy

For a linear coil, flux linkage is NΦB=LIN\Phi_B=LINΦB​=LI, defining self-inductance L in henries (H). Its emf is EL=−L dI/dt\mathcal E_L=-L\,dI/dtEL​=−LdI/dt. 2. Solve an LR transient

Inductor current cannot change instantly under finite voltage. For series R, L and ideal emf E\mathcal EE switched on at zero current, I(t)=ER(1−e−t/τ),τ=L/R.I(t)=\frac{\mathcal E}{R}(1-e^{-t/\tau}),\qquad\tau=L/R.I(t)=RE​(1−e−t/τ),τ=L/R.
