3. Write equations for unreducible networks

Choose branch-current directions; guesses may later be negative. At each independent junction, sum incoming current = sum outgoing current. Around each independent loop, sum potential changes = 0. Crossing a resistor with its current is −IR; against current is +IR. Crossing an ideal source from − to + is +E+\mathcal E+E. Solve the simultaneous equations and check power delivered equals power absorbed.
