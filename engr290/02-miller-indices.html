02 · Directions and Miller indices

Find crystal directions, plane intercepts, angles, and spacings.

Directions use square brackets

Subtract start coordinates from end coordinates in lattice units, clear fractions, and reduce to the smallest integer ratio. From (0,0,0) to (1/2,1/2,0), the direction is [110]. A negative component is conventionally written with an overbar. A family of equivalent directions uses angle brackets ⟨uvw⟩.

Planes use parentheses

Choose a plane that does not pass through the origin, find intercepts in units of a, b, c, take reciprocals, then clear fractions. An infinite intercept gives index zero. Intercepts a, 2b, ∞ give reciprocals 1, 1/2, 0 and indices (210). Braces {hkl} identify a family of planes.

Reverse the process

The conventional intercepts of (hkl) are a/h, b/k, c/l. A zero index means the plane is parallel to that axis. In a cubic crystal [hkl] is normal to (hkl); this simple identification does not generally hold in other crystal systems.

Cubic angles and spacing

For cubic directions, cos θ = (uu′ + vv′ + ww′)/[√(u²+v²+w²) √(u′²+v′²+w′²)]. For cubic plane spacing, dₕₖₗ = a/√(h²+k²+l²). Set the calculator to degrees when reporting angles.

WORKED EXAMPLE

For [110] and [111], the dot product is 2 and the lengths are √2 and √3. Thus θ = cos⁻¹(2/√6) = 35.26°. For (311), the intercepts are a/3, b, c.

Check your understanding

A cubic crystal has a = 0.400 nm. What is d₂₀₀, and which axis is parallel to the plane (210)?

Show the worked answer

d₂₀₀ = 0.400/√4 = 0.200 nm. The zero third index of (210) means the plane is parallel to z.
