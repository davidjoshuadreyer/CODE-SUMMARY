01 · Atomic structure and unit cells

Connect atomic arrangement to crystal geometry and density.

Atoms and electrons

Atomic number counts protons; a neutral atom has the same number of electrons. Valence electrons participate in bonding. An orbital holds at most two electrons with opposite spins. For oxygen, 1s² 2s² 2p⁴ accounts for all eight electrons.

From a lattice to a material

A lattice repeats through space; a crystal structure combines a lattice with its atomic basis. A unit cell describes the repeating geometry. A grain is a crystal region with a common orientation, and grain boundaries separate differently oriented grains. Amorphous solids lack long-range periodic order, although short-range order can remain.

Count shared atoms

A corner contributes 1/8 of an atom, a face contributes 1/2, and a body-centred atom contributes 1. Thus simple cubic has 1 atom per conventional cell, BCC has 2, and FCC has 4. For identical hard spheres: SC has a = 2r; BCC has √3a = 4r; FCC has √2a = 4r. Their coordination numbers are 6, 8, and 12 respectively.

Density and units

For a monatomic cubic crystal, ρ = nM/(Nₐa³). Here n is atoms per cell, M is molar mass, and Nₐ is Avogadro’s constant. Use a in cm with M in g/mol to obtain g/cm³. For a macroscopic sample use ρ = m/V directly; a mole-sized cube is not a unit cell.

WORKED EXAMPLE

One mole of MgO has mass 24.305 + 15.999 = 40.304 g. For the assignment cube, 22.37 mm = 2.237 cm. V = 2.237³ = 11.1943 cm³, so ρ = 40.304/11.1943 = 3.600 g/cm³. Always cube the converted length.

Check your understanding

An FCC material has M = 63.55 g/mol and a = 0.3615 nm. Using Nₐ = 6.023 × 10²³ mol⁻¹, estimate its density.

Show the worked answer

Convert a to 3.615 × 10⁻⁸ cm. Then ρ = 4(63.55)/[6.023 × 10²³(3.615 × 10⁻⁸)³] ≈ 8.934 g/cm³.
